Mr. Fang's Game Dev Studio

Game Name: zombie_hunter

Developer: Mr. Fang

Platform: Epic Games Store

Description: This is a casual, stress-relieving zombie-hunting game with no mandatory main quests. You can wander around freely to see if there are any surprises. When you spot zombies, you can shoot them—but watch out, they’ll charge at you! You can collect bounties for killed zombies at the police station. With the money, you can buy supplies, or go look for tanks to feel the power of these steel behemoths. The more zombies you kill, the higher your reputation rank, and the more tiered bounties you can claim. You’ll need money to take care of that big weapon. Once you’ve got it set up, you can fully experience its overwhelming power—you’re gonna love it!
